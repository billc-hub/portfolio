Origins & Global Track Record

Bill started his career at IBM and brings more than 30 years of global executive experience to his leadership based in Dubai. He has a long history of building internationally successful, high-growth businesses with a particular focus on growth and emerging markets.

His deep operational expertise spans Business Startup, Global Expansion, Strategic Planning, Strategic Marketing, High-Stakes Negotiation, Partner Management, and Go-to-Market Business Planning.

Leadership at Pan-Regional Scale

As the start-up Managing Director/CEO of MARA ISON, Bill led the launch and expansion of operations across 23 countries in the Middle East and Africa with a team of over 400 people operating across 18 local languages.

In addition, Bill has held numerous Board of Director and advisory board positions for leading Systems Integration, Managed Service Providers (MSPs), Business Process Outsourcing (BPO), and Value-Added Services (VAS) companies in Dubai, Egypt, Singapore, Philippines, India, Africa, the UK, and the USA.
